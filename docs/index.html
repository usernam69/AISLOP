AISLOP

AISLOP is an automation tool for the SubtleSpawns YouTube channel. Each day it turns a current technology news story into a short narrated video with captions and images, and uploads it to the channel as a YouTube Short.

What AISLOP does with your Google account

  • Uploads the channel's daily Shorts and sets their title, description, tags and visibility.
  • Adds each Short to a topic playlist on the channel.
  • Reads view, like and comment counts so the channel owner can see which videos do well.
  • Posts one question as the first comment on each new Short, and reads viewer comments so the owner can reply.

AISLOP is used only by the owner of the SubtleSpawns channel. It is not available to the public.
